Testing on an Android phone (read first)
  1. Open this page in Chrome from an https:// link, and allow the microphone when asked.
  2. Start with Words + sound. If it says the microphone is busy, or no words appear, switch Mode to Sound only (records, then checks when you press Stop) or Words only.
  3. Word checking sends your audio to Google's speech service, so it needs internet. Sound analysis runs on the phone.
  4. Use a quiet room, hold the phone about 20 cm away, recite the whole range, then press Stop.
  5. Reciter clips stream from the internet. Use the download buttons to send a recording and analysis back for tuning.
  6. This is an untested prototype on real voices. Treat flags as hints and confirm with a teacher.
What the sound analysis checks, and what it cannot
  • Madd length: natural (2 counts), lazim (6 counts), the stopping madd ('arid, 2/4/6, kept consistent) and short vowels stretched too long. Lengths are measured against your own short vowels, so your pace does not matter.
  • Heavy and light letters: the vowel next to a heavy letter (ص ط ض غ ق and a heavy ra') should sit lower in pitch-colour (second formant) than your own light vowels; the lam of Allah after a kasra should not. Needs a few light vowels as a baseline.
  • Ghunnah: a clear mim or nun (izhar, as in عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ and أَنْعَمْتَ) must stay short with no extra nasal sound.
  • Qalqalah: Al-Fatihah has no qalqalah, so nothing is checked here. A burst detector exists in the engine for other surahs but is not part of this prototype.
  • Not measured: exact articulation points (makharij) and the subtle letter attributes (sifat), hiding of the mim before waw (ikhfa), and ra' tarqiq next to a heavy letter. No recording-based method here can judge them reliably; compare with the reciter clips and a teacher.
  • Accuracy: the engine was tested on synthetic speech with known lengths and formants, not yet on real recitation. Expect false alarms and misses at first. Use headphones, a quiet room and a decent microphone. Lower the Sensitivity to Lenient if it flags correct recitation, and use the downloads to share a recording and analysis so thresholds can be tuned.
  • Learning: this is a practice aid, not a replacement for a qualified teacher (talaqqi).
